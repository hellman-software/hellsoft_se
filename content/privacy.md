## What data is processed, and why

### 1. Server logs (all Sites)

The Sites are hosted on **GitHub Pages**, operated by GitHub, Inc. When your browser requests a page, GitHub's servers receive and may temporarily log your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request, your browser type and the referring page. This is standard for any website and is needed to deliver the pages and to protect against abuse.

- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in serving the Sites securely.
- **Retention:** determined by GitHub. We do not receive or store these logs ourselves.
- **More information:** [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### 2. Web fonts (all Sites)

The Sites load typefaces from **Google Fonts**. To deliver a font file, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information. Google states that it does not use Fonts requests for profiling or advertising.

- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in consistent presentation of the Sites.
- **More information:** [Google Fonts privacy and data collection](https://developers.google.com/fonts/faq/privacy).

### 3. Advertising via Google AdSense (some Sites)

Some Sites display advertisements served by **Google AdSense**, operated by Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area. Google and its advertising partners may:

- set and read cookies and similar identifiers in your browser,
- process your IP address and device and browser information,
- use that data to select, deliver and measure advertisements, including personalised advertising where you have consented.

**Consent.** In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you will see a consent prompt before any advertising cookies are set. You can choose non-personalised ads or decline, and you can change your choice later through the link in the site footer or by clearing your browser's cookies.

**Your controls.** You can manage how Google personalises ads at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and opt out of interest-based advertising from many networks at [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu) or [aboutads.info](https://optout.aboutads.info).

- **Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised advertising and advertising cookies; legitimate interest for strictly necessary processing such as fraud prevention and basic ad delivery.
- **More information:** [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's advertising cookie types](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

### 4. Email contact

If you write to us, we process your name, email address and whatever you include in the message in order to reply and, where relevant, to prepare or deliver work you have asked about.

- **Legal basis:** legitimate interest in answering your enquiry (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps where the conversation concerns an engagement (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- **Retention:** for as long as the correspondence is relevant. Correspondence that forms part of a business relationship is kept as required by Swedish accounting law.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you,
- **rectify** inaccurate data,
- **erase** your data in certain circumstances,
- **restrict** or **object** to processing based on legitimate interest,
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal,
- **data portability** for data you provided to us,
- **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email [erik.hellman@hellsoft.se](mailto:erik.hellman@hellsoft.se). In practice, the only personal data we hold ourselves is email correspondence; data processed by Google or GitHub is best addressed through the controls linked above.

The supervisory authority in Sweden is the **Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection** (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), [imy.se](https://www.imy.se). You may also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

## Children

The Sites are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Google's advertising on the Sites is configured not to serve personalised ads to users Google identifies as under the age of consent.
